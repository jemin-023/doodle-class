# 2-minute judge demo

Open https://doodle-class.vercel.app

1. 0:00 Home. Say: "Doodle Class is a hand-writing cartoon teacher built on Sarvam: sarvam-105b, Bulbul v3, Saarika v2.5, in eight Indian languages."
2. 0:15 Pick Tara (Maths), type "Fractions", choose a language, start. Say: "The lesson streams. The board writes step one while the rest is still generating, with KaTeX equations and highlighted keywords."
3. 0:40 While Tara speaks, click "I have a doubt" and ask a question (typed or spoken). Say: "Saarika transcribes it and a new step is inserted right here."
4. 1:00 Finish the lesson, take the quiz, answer one by voice and one wrong on purpose. Say: "Voice answers are fuzzy-matched; wrong answers go to mistake memory."
5. 1:15 Home, then Talk to your teacher (Chintu). Turn on hands-free and speak. Say: "Voice to voice through /api/chat."
6. 1:30 Open Journey: show chapters, XP and a boss battle. Say: "Chapters unlock in order and end in a timed boss quiz."
7. 1:40 Syllabus: upload a PDF. Say: "pdf.js parses it in the browser and the model builds a personal book."
8. 1:50 Progress and Profile. Say: "Leitner spaced repetition, mastery, badges, and the coach's remedial lessons. With no API key everything falls back to offline samples."
